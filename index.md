## Research Interests

### 1. Social Media and Mental Health

- **Self-disclosure content** — analysis of the psychological content users disclose online (events, cognition, emotion).
- **Language of psychological support** — analysis of supportive texts in terms of psychological features and linguistic structure.
- **Impact of social media content on users** — effects at the user-group and engagement levels.

**Goal: knowledge mining, psychological support intervention, and platform management.**

### 2. Methods

- **Design science** — using mental health problem characteristics and theories (management and psychological theories) to drive methodological innovation (deep learning, LLM reasoning, multi-agent systems, etc.).
- **Data-driven knowledge discovery** — e.g., language-based support strategies and symptom co-occurrence.
