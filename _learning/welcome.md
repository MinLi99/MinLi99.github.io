## Why keep notes?

Reading a paper once is not the same as understanding it. These notes are where I
work things out in writing: derivations I had to redo, code that finally ran, and
the one sentence that made a confusing idea click.

They are study material, not polished writing — expect rough edges and gaps that
I fill in as I go.
